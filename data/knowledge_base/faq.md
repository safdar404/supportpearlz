# Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated 2026-04-05

## Ordering
**Can I order on the website and pay when it arrives?** Yes. Cash on delivery is available everywhere we deliver, after a confirmation call.
**What payment methods do you take?** Debit and credit cards, bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa and cash on delivery.
**Can I change my order after placing it?** You can change the address or product until the order is dispatched. Call the helpline or reply to your confirmation email.
**I didn't get an order confirmation.** Check spam. If nothing arrives within an hour, contact support with your phone number.
**Do you sell through shops too?** Yes, through authorised retail partners. Keep the shop's invoice for warranty.

## Delivery
**How fast will my purifier arrive?** Big cities usually get it in 1–2 working days; other cities 2–4; remote areas 5–7. See the Shipping Policy for the full list.
**Is delivery free?** Yes, on orders above PKR 10,000.
**Can I get it tomorrow?** Next-day delivery is available in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad if you order before 2 pm.
**The box looks damaged. What do I do?** Photograph it and note it on the courier's sheet before opening.

## Installation
**Do I need a plumber?** No. You can self-install using the Installation Guide or book a Pearlz technician.
**How much does professional installation cost?** PKR 1,500 in metro cities and PKR 2,500 elsewhere.
**Why does my water taste funny after installing?** New filters need flushing. Throw away the first two full tanks — the Installation Guide explains why.
**My water pressure is really low, will it work?** The purifier needs at least 10 psi. Below that you will see E-02 and may need a booster pump.

## Filters
**How often do I change the filters?** On the AquaPearl 500 Pro: sediment every 6 months, carbon and mineral every 12 months, RO membrane every 24 months, UV lamp every 12 months.
**My machine beeps three times — is it broken?** No, that is usually the pre-filter asking to be changed (E-03). Replace it and hold RESET for 5 seconds.
**Are filters covered by the warranty?** No. Filters wear out with use and count as consumables.
**Can I use cheaper non-Pearlz filters?** We don't recommend it. Damage caused by non-Pearlz parts is not covered.
**Where do I buy replacement filters?** On our website or from authorised partners. The Pricing Guide lists every SKU.
**Is there a bundle for a year of filters?** Yes, the Year of Pure Water bundle for the AquaPearl 500 Pro.

## Warranty and returns
**How long is the warranty?** 24 months for the AquaPearl 500 Pro, 12 months for the AquaPearl 300 and 18 months for the AeroPearl Mini.
**When does my warranty start — delivery day?** No, it starts on the invoice date.
**If I sell my purifier, does the warranty go with it?** Yes, once, with the original invoice.
**How long do I have to return something?** 14 days from delivery. Conditions apply — see the Refund & Returns Policy.
**I opened the box but didn't install it. Can I still return it?** Usually yes, with all packaging, minus a 10% restocking fee.
**How do I make a warranty claim?** Through the app, the helpline or warranty@pearlzhome.example. Have your invoice and serial number ready.

## App and account
**Which phones does the app support?** Android 9+ and iOS 15+.
**The app won't connect to my purifier.** Turn on Bluetooth and location, update the firmware to 2.4 or later, and hold WIFI for 3 seconds.
**What does the app track?** Water usage, filter life, TDS and alerts. See the Privacy Policy.
**How do I delete my account?** Email privacy@pearlzhome.example. We complete deletion within 30 days.
**I forgot my password.** Use "Forgot password" on the login screen; a reset link is sent by SMS.

## AeroPearl Mini
**What size room is the AeroPearl Mini for?** Up to 250 square feet.
**How often do I change its filter?** Every 9 months, or when the red ring lights up.
**Is it noisy?** About 28 dB on sleep mode.
