# Pearlz Home Systems — Warranty Policy

Version 3.1 · Last updated 2026-03-01 · Applies to purchases made in Pakistan through the Pearlz online store and authorised retail partners.

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains what Pearlz Home Systems (Pvt.) Ltd. ("Pearlz") repairs or replaces free of charge when a product develops a fault, how long that protection lasts, and what is not covered. It should be read together with the Service & Maintenance Agreement, which describes how a claim is actually processed.

## 2. Warranty periods
### 2.1 Standard warranty by product line
- **AquaPearl 500 Pro:** 24 months from the date of purchase.
- **AquaPearl 300:** 12 months from the date of purchase.
- **AeroPearl Mini:** 18 months from the date of purchase.

### 2.2 How the warranty period is calculated
The warranty period starts on the **invoice date**, not on the delivery date or the installation date. If an order is delivered several days after it is invoiced, the warranty still starts on the invoice date. Customers who bought through a retail partner should keep the partner's invoice, because the invoice date printed on it is the date Pearlz uses.

### 2.3 Discontinued models
The AquaPearl 200 (discontinued in 2024) and the AeroPearl 1 (discontinued in 2023) remain covered for the remainder of their original warranty period. Pearlz has also extended parts-only coverage for the AquaPearl 200 pump and control board until 31 December 2027. Labour for discontinued models is charged at the standard service rate once the original warranty period has ended. Replacement filters for discontinued models will remain on sale until stocks are exhausted.

## 3. Coverage
### 3.1 What is covered
For the warranty period above, Pearlz will repair or, at its discretion, replace the following parts if they fail under normal domestic use:
- the purifier body and housing (manufacturing defects only, such as a cracked seam present at delivery or a housing that splits without impact);
- the booster pump and motor;
- the electronics, including the control board, display, sensors and power adapter;
- for AeroPearl Mini, the fan motor and control electronics.

### 3.2 Exclusions
The warranty does **not** cover:
1. **Consumables.** Filter cartridges (sediment pre-filter, carbon block, RO membrane, mineral cartridge, HEPA filter) and the UV lamp are consumables. They wear out through normal use and are excluded from the standard warranty.
2. **Physical damage.** Damage caused by impact, dropping, fire, flooding, pests or improper transport by the customer.
3. **Unauthorised repair.** Any product that has been opened, modified or repaired by anyone other than a Pearlz-authorised technician. Opening the unit voids the warranty for the whole product, even if the fault seems unrelated.
4. **Commercial use.** Use in offices, restaurants, shops or any setting serving more than one household.
5. **Water-quality damage.** Damage caused by feed water outside the specification in the product manual, for example total dissolved solids (TDS) above 2,000 ppm or inlet pressure above 60 psi without a pressure regulator.
6. **Installation not following the Installation Guide**, including the self-installation conditions listed in that guide.
7. **Consequential damage.** Pearlz' liability under this warranty is limited to repair or replacement of the product itself. Damage to other property, such as kitchen cabinets, flooring or walls, is not covered by this warranty.

## 4. Transfer on resale
The warranty can be transferred once to a new owner in Pakistan if the original invoice is provided. The remaining warranty period continues from the original invoice date; it does not restart.

## 5. How to make a claim
Warranty claims are processed under the claim procedure in **Section 6 of the Service & Maintenance Agreement**. You will need the invoice, the unit's serial number (printed on the label inside the front service door) and a short description of the fault. Claims cannot be accepted without the serial number.

## 6. Repaired and replaced parts
Parts repaired or replaced under warranty are covered for the remainder of the original warranty period or 3 months, whichever is longer.
