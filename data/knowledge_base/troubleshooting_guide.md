# Pearlz Troubleshooting Guide — AquaPearl and AeroPearl

Version 4.0 · Last updated 2026-02-10 · Applies to firmware 2.4 and later unless stated.

## How to use this guide
Find the symptom, check the probable cause, and follow the action. If a symptom appears under **Escalation criteria**, stop and contact support — do not attempt the fix yourself.

## Audible Alert Codes
| Sound / light | Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| One long beep | E-01 | Storage tank full | Normal. No action needed. |
| Two short beeps | E-02 | Low inlet water pressure | Check that the inlet valve is fully open. If the mains pressure is low, run the unit later or install a booster. |
| Three short beeps, red LED blinking | E-03 | Sediment pre-filter saturated | Replace the sediment pre-filter cartridge, then press and hold RESET for 5 seconds to clear the alert. |
| Four short beeps | E-04 | RO membrane efficiency low (output TDS high) | Book a membrane check; the RO membrane may need replacement. |
| Continuous beeping | E-05 | Internal leak detected (firmware 2.4+) | Close the inlet valve, unplug the unit and contact support. |
| Beep every 10 seconds | E-06 | Pump overheating | Unplug for 30 minutes. If it repeats, contact support. |
| Rapid beeping, amber LED | E-07 | TDS sensor fault | Contact support. |
| Two long beeps, blue LED off | E-08 | UV lamp fault (firmware 2.4+) | Replace the UV lamp; contact support if the alert persists. |

## Symptom table
| Symptom | Probable cause | Action |
|---|---|---|
| No power, display dark | Adapter unplugged, socket off, tripped breaker | Check the socket with another appliance; reseat the adapter. |
| Low water flow from tap | Clogged pre-filter, low inlet pressure | Replace pre-filter if older than 6 months; check E-02. |
| Water leaking at the base | Loose quick-connect fitting, cracked tank seal | Close inlet valve, unplug, push fittings fully in. If leak continues, see escalation. |
| Bad or unusual taste | New filter not flushed, carbon block exhausted | After any filter change, run the first-run flushing procedure in the Installation Guide. Replace carbon block if older than 12 months. |
| Unit runs continuously and never stops | Tank valve fault, very low pressure | Check E-02; if the pump never stops, contact support. |
| Three beeps | E-03 sediment pre-filter saturated | See Audible Alert Codes. |
| Blinking red LED | Filter change due | Replace the indicated filter and reset. |
| TDS reading high | Membrane wear or high feed TDS | Compare with feed water; see E-04. |
| Noisy pump | Air in lines after filter change, low pressure | Run for 10 minutes to purge air. Persistent grinding noise: contact support. |
| App will not pair | Phone Bluetooth off, firmware below 2.4 | Enable Bluetooth and location; update firmware; hold WIFI for 3 seconds to re-enter pairing mode. |
| Water slightly cloudy then clears | Micro-bubbles after filter change | Normal; clears within 2 minutes in the glass. |
| Plastic smell from new unit | Manufacturing residue | Flush per Installation Guide. |
| Display shows "--" | Sensor warming up | Wait 5 minutes after power-on. |
| AeroPearl Mini red ring | HEPA filter due | Replace HEPA filter and hold the fan button for 5 seconds. |
| AeroPearl Mini loud fan | Filter clogged or turbo mode | Replace filter; check fan speed setting. |
| Water pooling under the sink cabinet | Drain line not secured | Close the inlet valve and contact support if a fitting is cracked. |

## Escalation criteria
Do **not** attempt the following yourself — contact support (helpline 0800-PEARLZ, support@pearlzhome.example):
- any burning or electrical smell;
- water leaking near the power adapter or socket;
- E-05 (internal leak) or E-07 (TDS sensor fault);
- E-06 occurring more than twice in a day;
- any fault that would require opening the purifier body. Opening the unit voids the warranty.
