# Pearlz Home Systems — Shipping Policy

Version 2.4 · Last updated 2026-01-15

## Delivery Timelines
Delivery times are counted in working days (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays) from the day the order is dispatched.

| Region tier | Cities | Standard delivery | Express / next-day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metro | Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi | 1–2 working days | Next-day delivery available in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad only |
| Secondary city | Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Hyderabad, Sialkot, Gujranwala | 2–4 working days | Not available |
| Remote area | Gilgit, Skardu, Hunza, Chitral, Gwadar, Turbat, Zhob and other remote districts | 5–7 working days | Not available |

Next-day delivery costs PKR 450 and must be selected at checkout. It is offered only in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad. Rawalpindi receives standard metro delivery.

## Dispatch cut-off times
Orders placed and paid before **1:00 pm** on a working day are dispatched the same day. Next-day delivery orders must be placed before **2:00 pm**. Cash-on-delivery orders are dispatched after phone confirmation, which can add up to one working day.

## Shipping charges
Standard shipping is free on orders above **PKR 10,000**. Orders below that amount pay a flat PKR 300 delivery charge in metro and secondary cities and PKR 550 in remote areas.

## Packaging and fragile items
Purifiers are shipped upright in double-walled cartons with foam inserts. Glass and acrylic accessories are packed separately. Please inspect the carton in front of the courier. If the carton is visibly crushed or wet, note it on the courier's delivery sheet and photograph it before opening; this protects your right to a replacement for transit damage.

## Failed delivery attempts
The courier makes two further delivery attempts after a failed first attempt. If all attempts fail, the parcel returns to our Lahore warehouse and a re-shipping fee of PKR 350 applies. Cash-on-delivery orders that are refused at the door may be restricted to prepaid payment for future orders.

## International shipping
Pearlz does not currently ship outside Pakistan.

## Tracking
A tracking link is sent by SMS and email when the order is dispatched. Tracking updates may take up to 12 hours to appear for remote-area shipments.
